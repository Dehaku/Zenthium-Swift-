# Missions

## Start sequence
1. Camera zooms in on the mission goal and the mission text displays.
2. Camera zooms out and pans over.
3. Camera zooms in on the player hero.

## Types
| Type | Goal |
|---|---|
| Rescue | Enter a location and extract citizens |
| Countdown | A bomb or horror egg is about to go off or hatch. Find and break it before time runs out |
| Arson Riot | Goons are setting buildings and vehicles on fire |
| Invasion | A large wave of goons approaches to take the location. Defeat all of them |

## Shared rules
- Goons are scattered around every mission and fight the player on sight.
- A mission has a chance to be led by a super villain (a faction Leader, `factions.md`).
- Each mission gets one random **optional secondary goal**, for example:
  - Smash an egg nest.
  - Rescue a scientist.
  - Show off for a news reporter (use a power or gear in front of them).

## Citizens
| Type | Behavior |
|---|---|
| Scared | Panics and flees from threats |
| Incapacitated | Cannot move on their own |
| Stubborn | Won't move; harder to extract |

## Extraction
- **Grab & Haul:** the player grabs a citizen and pulls them along behind. One method for all extraction by hand.
- **Extraction gear:** special equipment, such as a Metal Gear-style balloon or a teleportation device.
