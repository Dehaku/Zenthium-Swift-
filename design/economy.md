# Economy

## Zenny
- Crystals in many colors. Their lore is in `lore.md`.
- Players start with no Zenny. All of it is earned from missions.
- Three currencies, one per family. Colors within a family are looks only.

| Family | Rarity | Colors | Buys |
|---|---|---|---|
| Basic | Common | Red, Blue, Purple, White | Stats (Training Room) |
| Gem | Uncommon | Crimson Ruby, Sapphire, Amethyst, Diamond | Modules: buying and upgrading (Zen Garden, Research Lab). Higher tiers cost more |
| Metallic | Rare | Gold, Silver | Slot unlocks and hero unlocks. Also cosmetics, melting down, and mission tips and intel reveals (`hq.md`) |

- Each mission type rewards its own mix of families.
- Zenny is awarded on the end-of-mission screen (`hud.md`). Bigger rewards show as bigger crystals, since Zenny melds with its own kind.

### Metallic sinks
Keep Metallic useful after every slot and hero is unlocked:
- **Cosmetics:** costumes, color palettes and alternate looks for heroes and Recruits.
- **Melting down:** 1 Metallic becomes 10 Basic or 4 Gem.
- **Hiring Recruits:** the first Recruit is free; each extra one costs Metallic.

## Hero reward bonus
- Multiplies all mission rewards. Tracked per hero.
- **All reward bonuses add together:** hero bonus, rank, hidden intel, secondary goals and Story Arcs. Example: a hero at 130% (+30%) on a rank B mission (+50%) in a Story Arc (+100%) earns 100% + 30% + 50% + 100% = 280%.
- There is no XP. All progression comes from Zenny.
- +10% for each mission played without that hero. No cap.
- When the hero completes a mission, their bonus drops by 30 points, to a floor of 100%. Example: 360% → 330%.
- A failed mission doesn't change any bonus.

## Dev options
- Reward bonus drop amount (default 30 points).
- Toggle: the bonus resets to 100% whenever the hero completes a mission.
