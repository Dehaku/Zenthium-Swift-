# Heroes and Create-a-Hero (CAH)

## Preset heroes
- The roster isn't capped.
- Each preset has a full, fixed kit: 4 active, 4 passive and 4 gear modules (`modules.md`). Players can't swap them.
- Only the first slot of each type is unlocked at the start. The rest unlock with Zenny (`economy.md`), in the order listed below.
- Unlocked at the start: Rush Hour, Major Overclock and Rune.
- Unplayable for now: the HQ cast, Shera, Tia and Seria (`lore.md`).
- The rest are unlocked with Zenny, or through milestones, achievements or story beats.
- Madame Tarot only unlocks by finishing the Relic Fever arc (`StoryArcs/RelicFever.md`). She can't be bought.
- Stat shorthand: high = 20, low = 6, unlisted = 10 (the base, `stats.md`).

## Create-a-Hero (CAH)
- Each CAH is called a **Recruit**. Players can make multiple Recruits (extra ones cost Metallic Zenny, `economy.md`). Story-wise they're all the same character, one SWIFT recruit; having several lets players try builds without resetting.
- CAH slots start empty and can take any module the player has unlocked.

## Slots
| Slot | Start | Max | Holds |
|---|---|---|---|
| Active power | 1 | 4 | Active modules |
| Passive power | 1 | 4 | Passive modules |
| Gear | 1 | 4 | Items, armor, weapons, spellcasting items, utility belt, iconic gear (Thor's hammer, a power ring) |

- Most slots start locked. They are unlocked with Zenny (`economy.md`).
- Every module takes 1 slot. A hero can't equip two copies of the same module.
- Kick, Drag and Sprint are always available outside the slots (`modules.md`).
- Loadouts change only at HQ (`hq.md`).
- Stats, slots and upgrades are tracked per hero.
