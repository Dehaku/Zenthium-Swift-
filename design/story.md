# Story Arcs

- Over time, chains of Story Arc missions appear on the mission map (`hq.md`). They lightly change the map's look and nothing else.
- Several arcs can run at once. Each pops up a unique mission with some plot.
- An arc can be run by any faction that lists it in `factions.md`.

## Arcs

### Relic Fever
A faction steals increasingly valuable magic items. Draft chain:
1. A monkey's paw, from an antique shop.
2. Pandora's box, from a museum.
3. The Holy Grail, from a private collector's vault.
4. Finale: the Ark of the Covenant.
