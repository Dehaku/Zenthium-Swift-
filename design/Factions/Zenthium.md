# Zenthium (neutral)

Hostile crystalline creatures from Zenthium (`../lore.md`) that come through a Zenthium Fissure (`../difficulty.md`). They attack everyone.

Shared faction rules are in `../factions.md`.

## Faction rules
- **Neutral:** they attack anything not on their own team, with no preference (`../enemies.md`). They don't loot Valuables; they want blood and souls, not money.
- **Spawning:** only from a Zenthium Fissure, on the timing in `../enemies.md`. They ignore the population cap and never run.
- **Anchored:** Zenthium entities within 15 m of an open Fissure take 50% less damage. Closing the Fissure removes this.
- **Closing reward:** destroying the Fissure counts as a completed bonus secondary goal: +25% rewards (`../missions.md`).
- **Not enemies for goals:** they never count toward "defeat every enemy" (`../missions.md`).
- **Leader behavior:** the Zen Commander guards the Fissure, staying within 20 m of it. Once the Fissure is closed, it hunts the nearest target.
- **Kits** are themed on Zenny colors (`../lore.md`): red gravity, crimson blood, amethyst soul.
- **Looks:** each Zenthium entity is tinted a random Zenny color. Flavor only.
- **Comic callouts:** crystalline sounds instead of words: "KRRRNCH!", "SHRIIING!", "HMMMMMM..."
- **Story Arcs:** none yet.
