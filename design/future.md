# Future Features

Ideas board. We won't build these now, and nothing else in the design depends on them.

- **Hero's Journey:** a push-your-luck mode. Complete a chain of missions with growing rewards; fail one and lose it all. Like Payday 2's Crime Spree.
- **Territory:** several cities, or sections of one city, to protect. Losing a mission loses that territory.
- **Difficulty hazards:** higher difficulties add map hazards such as falling buildings and downed power lines.
- **Sharper eyes:** enemies' awareness bars fill faster at higher ranks, making stealth harder.
- **Mixed factions:** missions with several factions that also fight each other.
- **Control rebinding:** including combos such as Shift+1 or Ctrl+RMB.
- **Multiplayer.**
- **Returning Valuables:** bring a civilian's dropped Valuable back to them for a small reward and a Meeting Room line such as "returned my wallet, a true hero".
- **Zenny-themed modules:** module ideas based on the Zenny colors' lore (`lore.md`):
  - Crimson Ruby: immune to damage for a few seconds, then the stored damage is applied over time.
  - Sapphire rounds: +50% damage and a chance to stun.
  - Red rounds: impacts pull nearby things inward.
  - Blue rounds: add elemental damage.
  - Purple pulses: stun, slow, charm or confuse.
  - Amethyst auras (SoulFire, DiamondSoul, EsperSoul, LostSoul): guaranteed hits on one kind of action.
- **Loot fights:** goons from different factions fight each other over Valuables (with Mixed factions).
