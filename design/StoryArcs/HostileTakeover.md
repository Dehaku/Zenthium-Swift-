# Hostile Takeover

The opening arc. On the heroes' first day at SWIFT, Shera's orientation tour is interrupted: the Galactic Repo Agency claims her tower, and half her property, were never paid for, and starts repossessing them.

Story Arc rules are in `../story.md`.

- **Factions:** Galactic Repo Agency only.
- **Minimum rank:** D. Starts automatically as the game's first mission. After that, it can come back through the normal arc roll (`../story.md`).
- **Completion unlock:** Sundown Sal (`../heroes.md`).
- **Named entities:** GleebPeep (the Leader, `../factions.md`), Seria (VIP in stage 4b).

## Plot sequences
**Stage 1 briefing**
> **Seria:** And this is the Zen Garden, and over here is the Research Lab, and—
> **Tia:** Stop. There are crop circles appearing in front of one of our buildings.
> **Shera:** Mine, specifically. The tour's over. Go remind them who owns this city.

**Stage 1 success**
> **Seria:** Everyone's safe! Great first day!
> **Tia:** It isn't over. The same signal is coming from one of Shera's banks.
> **Shera:** Of course it is.

**Stage 1 failure**
> **Shera:** That was... a start. We'll call it a learning experience.

**Stage 4 briefing**
> **Tia:** They've landed in our lobby.
> **Shera:** In my lobby. Brats, the lot of them. Throw them out.

**Finale success**
> **Seria:** The mothership's leaving! And someone's waving a hat at it?
> **Sundown Sal:** Those varmints took my horse in '82. Been waitin' forty years for that.
> **Shera:** She shoots straight and holds a grudge. Tia, get her a contract.

**Finale failure**
> **Tia:** The tower is gone. Temporarily, I'm assured.
> **Shera:** We'll be getting it back. I keep my receipts.
