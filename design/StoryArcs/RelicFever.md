# Relic Fever

Story Arc rules are in `../story.md`.
A faction steals increasingly powerful magic relics.

- **Factions:** Hexbound Coven or Galactic Repo Agency.
- **Minimum rank:** C.
- **Completion unlock:** Madame Tarot (`../heroes.md`). Her magic deck fits right in.
- **Relics** are named Valuables placed by the stage: carried, dropped and stolen like any Valuable (`world.md`).

| # | Stage | Mission | Location | Fixed fields | Checkpoints | Success | Failure |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Paw | Invasion | Antique shop | Secondary goal: Prevent 50% of Valuables from being stolen. Relic: the Monkey's Paw | An enemy picks up the Paw: plot sequence, and a new goal: recover the Paw | Stage 2 | Stage 2, with Night Time ("They wished for endless night") |
| 2 | Don't Open It | Countdown: the thieves are prying the box open | Museum | Relic: Pandora's Box. Leader present | Timer at 50%: the Leader arrives early, with a plot sequence | Stage 3 | Stage 3, with a Zenthium Fissure ("Something crawled out of the box") |
| 3 | The Collector | Rescue. Named VIP: Lord Pemberton, the collector | Collector's vault (a mansion) | Secondary goal: Prevent 50% of Valuables from being stolen | Pemberton rescued: plot sequence ("They already have the Grail!"), and a named courier carrying the Grail spawns and runs for the map edge. New goal: stop the courier | Stage 4 | Stage 3b |
| 3b | Ransom | Rescue: Pemberton has been kidnapped | Warehouse | Leader present | Pemberton rescued: plot sequence | Stage 4 | Stage 4, with Night Time |
| 4 | The Ark (finale) | Invasion | Warehouse ("government storage, don't ask") | Leader present. Map size: Large | The Leader drops to 50% HP: plot sequence, and the Leader flees with the Ark. If the Leader escapes, the mission fails | Arc complete | Arc ends |

## Sample plot sequences
A line marked *(Neutral)* plays by default. The faction lines right after it replace it when that faction is running the stage (`../story.md`).

**Stage 1 briefing**
> **Seria:** Ooh, an antique shop break-in! Somebody's after the Monkey's Paw!
> **Tia:** A cursed relic. It grants wishes, at a cost. No one should use it.
> **Tia** *(Neutral)*: Whoever they are, they won't stop at one relic.
> **Tia** *(Hexbound Coven)*: The Coven. They believe every relic they steal makes their spells stronger. They're right.
> **Seria** *(Galactic Repo Agency)*: It's the Repo Agency! They say Earth's six thousand years behind on its payments!
> **Shera:** Then go keep it in its dusty little box. Quietly.

**Stage 1 success**
> **Seria:** Paw secured! ...Can I make just one tiny wish?
> **Tia:** No.
> **Shera** *(Neutral)*: Whoever's behind this is welcome to file a complaint.
> **Seria** *(Hexbound Coven)*: Vex is gonna be sooo mad. Overdue fines for everyone!
> **Shera** *(Galactic Repo Agency)*: GleebPeep is welcome to file a complaint.
> **Shera:** They'll go for the museum next, I expect. Pandora's box. Try not to open it.

**Stage 1 failure**
> **Tia:** They used the Paw. Their wish: endless night over the museum district.
> **Tia** *(Neutral)*: And they'll do everything they can to keep it that way.
> **Tia** *(Hexbound Coven)*: The Coven has already begun a ritual to keep it that way.
> **Seria** *(Galactic Repo Agency)*: A Grey wrote the wish on a clipboard! Apparently that counts!
> **Shera:** How dramatic. Bring a flashlight.

**Finale success**
> **Shera:** The Ark stays where it is. Good. I'd hate to redecorate.
> **Shera** *(Neutral)*: Do pass my regards to the thieves.
> **Shera** *(Hexbound Coven)*: Tell the Librarian her overdue book is staying on my shelf.
> **Shera** *(Galactic Repo Agency)*: Inform GleebPeep the Ark has been declared non-repossessable.

**Finale failure**
> **Seria:** They got away with the Ark!
> **Tia** *(Neutral)*: With the Ark, they're far more dangerous now.
> **Tia** *(Hexbound Coven)*: With the Ark in Vex's hands, the Coven's spells will be... considerable.
> **Seria** *(Galactic Repo Agency)*: It's going on their mothership! Do they even have a shelf big enough?!
> **Shera:** Then they'll be back. Thieves always come back for more.
